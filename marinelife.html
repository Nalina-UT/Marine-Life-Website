Sea Turtle

Sea turtles are ancient ocean travellers. They migrate across thousands of miles and return to coastal beaches to lay their eggs.

Habitat: Tropical oceans Diet: Seagrass and jellyfish
MAMMAL

Dolphin

Dolphins are highly intelligent marine mammals known for their social behaviour, communication and playful nature.

Habitat: Coastal and open oceans Diet: Fish and squid
FISH

Great White Shark

Great white sharks are powerful ocean predators that help maintain balance within marine food chains.

Habitat: Coastal waters Diet: Fish and marine mammals
MAMMAL

Blue Whale

The blue whale is the largest animal known to have lived on Earth, yet it mainly feeds on tiny creatures called krill.

Habitat: Open ocean Diet: Krill
MOLLUSC

Giant Pacific Octopus

Octopuses are intelligent animals that can solve problems, change colour and squeeze through remarkably small spaces.

Habitat: Rocky ocean floors Diet: Crabs, fish and shellfish
FISH

Clownfish

Clownfish live safely among sea anemones. Both animals benefit from this special relationship.

Habitat: Coral reefs Diet: Algae and plankton
