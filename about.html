ABOUT OUR OCEAN

The Heart of Our Planet

The ocean covers more than 70% of the Earth's surface and is home to an incredible variety of marine life. From colourful coral reefs to mysterious deep-sea environments, every part of the ocean supports a unique ecosystem.

It also helps regulate the climate, produces much of the oxygen we breathe and provides food and livelihoods for communities around the world.

OUR MISSION

Inspiring Ocean Conservation

Our mission is to encourage people to explore, understand and care for the ocean. Through education and awareness, Sea World celebrates marine biodiversity and promotes actions that help reduce pollution, protect wildlife and preserve ocean habitats.
